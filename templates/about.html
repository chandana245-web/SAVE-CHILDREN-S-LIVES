About

Built around trust, transparency and care

Save Children’s Lives is a healthcare-tech prototype focused on AI-assisted medical review, hospital confirmation and transparent crowdfunding for pediatric care.

Families can share treatment needs, documents and milestones. Donors can review verified cases with clear progress data, and hospitals can validate case details without exposing personal information publicly.

Every campaign and donation in this prototype is labeled DEMO DATA. It is designed to demonstrate trust-centered workflows, not to process real medical or financial information.
